CH.05 THE RULEBOOK

KNOW THE CODE.

Keep it original, keep it respectful and keep moving.

Entry 013Articles of the build
  1. 01

    Team size

    Crews of 2–4. Solo builders can be matched at the venue.

  2. 02

    Eligibility

    Open to college students from every year and branch. Bring valid ID.

  3. 03

    Submission

    Submit a public repository, working demo and five-slide pitch.

  4. 04

    Technology

    Any stack is welcome. Open-source libraries and APIs are fair game.

  5. 05

    Originality

    Build during the event. Declare any boilerplate you start with.

  6. 06

    Deadlines

    Register by 5 Sep, submit the concept by 11 Sep and demo on 13 Sep.

  7. 07

    Conduct

    Respect every participant. Harassment and plagiarism end the run.

Play fair. Build fearless.
